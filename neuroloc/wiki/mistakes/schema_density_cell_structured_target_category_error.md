# schema density cell structured target category error

status: historical context only. frozen as of 2026-05-09. do not edit.

## summary

the schema-density cell was briefly documented as a strict-density compression repair, but the target was structured by construction. that does not satisfy the user's high-density knowledge-compression request.

## what happened

`local_100k_schema_density_cell` generated values and provenance from compact modular schema coefficients. the simulation then fitted those coefficients and recovered many held-out facts exactly.

this produced high strict density, but it compressed a formula, not unplanned knowledge. the useful content was in the structure of the generator. the test therefore answered a different question from the one requested.

## why it matters

if the fact family is structured, the compression win can come from the author choosing a low-description-length world. that does not prove a neuron-cell can store substantially more arbitrary knowledge than an ordinary baseline.

the correct target must avoid hand-designed formulas, preplanned value functions, template leakage, per-fact tables, uncharged side channels, and schema labels that make the answer recoverable by construction.

## prevention

future high-density knowledge-compression attempts must use unstructured or unknown-structure facts. any discovered structure must be learned from data under held-out controls, charged in the accounting, and compared against sparse read, verbatim storage, product-key memory, and random-label controls.

structured formula compression may remain as a boundary or negative-control artifact, but it must not be promoted as the requested compression result.

this rule is now also written into `AGENTS.md` under the high-density knowledge gate. future agents must read it before attempting any high-density compression claim.
