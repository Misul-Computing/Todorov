# schema density cell boundary

status: historical context only. frozen as of 2026-05-09. do not edit.

## role

this page records the claim boundary for [[tests/local_100k_schema_density_cell]]. after user correction, the result is demoted further: structured formula compression is not the requested high-density knowledge-compression target.

## boundary

independent exact associative facts carry their own entropy. if the value and provenance labels are independent of the key and of each other, exact retrieval requires enough stored information to identify those labels. moving that information from trainable weights into runtime dictionaries, tables, examples, seeds, or schema side channels is not strict compression unless those bits are charged.

the previous high-density cell [[tests/local_100k_high_density_cell]] made this visible. its params-only density cleared the 600x target, but strict density fell to `6.1709981167608285x` after charging committed state.

the new schema-density cell changes the fact family. value and provenance labels are no longer independent random labels. they are generated by a compact modular schema that can be fitted from a small number of examples, committed as coefficients, and reused over many held-out keys.

therefore the only surviving claim is a boundary claim:

```text
a compact schema-generative cell can preserve exact associative retrieval over a structured fact family at strict density above the 600x target, but this is formula compression and does not count as the requested high-density knowledge-compression result.
```

the rejected claim remains:

```text
the same cell compresses arbitrary independent exact facts by 600x.
```

the rejected project-target claim is:

```text
structured formula compression solves the high-density neuron-cell knowledge target.
```

## controls

required controls for this claim:

- train/test key split has no overlap.
- no per-fact committed value, provenance, or commit rows exist.
- schema-disabled, shuffled-schema, decoder-disabled, write-disabled, read-disabled, no-memory, recency-only, shuffled-key, shuffled-value, and shuffled-provenance controls collapse.
- independent random-label control fails held-out exact retrieval.
- strict density reports trainable parameters, committed schema bits, decoder bits, key-schema bits, and training-supervision bits.
- authorization flags keep general independent-fact breakthrough, full nm, arbitrary chat, external simulator, and paid compute at `0.0`.
