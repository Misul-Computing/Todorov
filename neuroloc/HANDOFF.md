## Latest local session

The latest recorded work is the local CLS trainer session from 2026-08-12 to
2026-08-13. It defined a Todorov-owned MLX Metal trainer for an Apple M5 Pro,
using byte-level FineWeb-Edu, a private gated-delta fast path, and a
frozen-trunk specialist opened through a zero-initialized scalar gate. The
first model pattern is `[attn, delta, delta, delta]` at sequence length `512`,
attention window `128`, width `768`, `12` layers, `12` heads, and head width
`64`.

The local results show a working fit path and useful small hybrid and role
probes, but they do not establish 100M-class long-range passkey learning, 1B
parity, a general CLS, or a public model release. The trainer, checkpoints,
datasets, and result registry remain local-only and are excluded from the
public source push. The exact session record is
`neuroloc/wiki/tests/todorov_cls_macbook_session_202608.md`.
